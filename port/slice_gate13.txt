# Camera::Behavior runs the 19-state machine, Camera::Render builds the
# projection and view matrices and pushes them at the geometry engine, and
# GetAngleToCamera reads the heading the camera itself published.
#

# cannot take verbatim lives in port/unmatched/ instead and is listed in

# G3i::PerspectiveW_ / G3i::LookAt_ (register-bound MMIO pointers, hostgen
# MMIO_PTR; PerspectiveW_'s DIVCNT seam is an MMIO_EXTERN entry),
# Camera::Render, LenVec3 and cstd::ldiv_result go through hostgen
